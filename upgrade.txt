Upgrade notes for local_parce
=============================

Current development pair (2026-08-08)
=====================================

* Requires Moodle 5.1, PHP 8.2 or newer and aiprovider_bbco 2026080800.
* Deploy aiprovider_bbco before local_parce.
* Active conversations and AI prompt history now come exclusively from MODE_SESSION cache.
* Database tables are durable history, guest audit and AI-action traces only.
* chatid stores the canonical course or system context ID.
* conversationkey is a cryptographically random thread identifier independent of PHP session IDs.
* Active threads rotate at their configured limits, with hard maxima of 40 complete turns and 16,000 estimated tokens.
* Prompt history, retrieved data and complete payload budgets are 8,000, 8,000 and 18,000 estimated tokens respectively.
* Successful guest turns and AI traces are persisted for audit; guests cannot query persistent history.
* Privacy API export/deletion covers conversations and AI traces and invalidates active cache generations.
* The obsolete cache_ttl setting has been removed. Session lifetime and rollover limits define the active lifecycle.
* Parce instructions replace the effective provider's generic generate_text system instruction for each request.
* Retrieved Search and Calendar content remains untrusted data delimited inside the separate user prompt.
* There is no scheduled retention or purge. History and traces are provisionally excluded from course backup/restore.

For this development workspace, unsupported older installations are replaced with a fresh uninstall/reinstall. No production upgrade execution is part of this release validation.

Third-party Markdown library
============================

Marked.js v11.1.1 remains registered under lib/marked with its MIT licence. Server-side Moodle formatting and cleaning is the security boundary for returned Markdown.
